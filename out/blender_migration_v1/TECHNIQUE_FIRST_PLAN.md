# Technique-first migration

Owner direction: stop serial approval stops for repeated techniques. First exercise
the exceptional techniques, carry their lessons into autonomous batches, and
present useful review packages. This is not permission to change accepted timing,
performances, effect choreography or to start paid generation/mastering.

## Exceptional queue

1. **World reveal, cut 26:** separate the woman/lantern from the garden, fade the
   rectangular background earlier, shrink the silhouette into the globe, retain
   sphere texture, nebula, orbital occlusion and incoming signals. Inspect matte
   edges, optical transition and the large change in scale at 720 and 1080.
   The accepted source is a still with an authored silhouette, not a tracked
   moving-person cutout or physically continuous camera geography.
2. **Shop snap zoom, cut 29:** two independently timed performances, 17 spatial
   shutter samples, a hard switch hidden inside radial streak/defocus, and an
   overshoot settling on the close-up. No face dissolve. Wide origin 2387,
   close origin 2425, switch 2431, push 2424..2436, signal clear at 2433.
   First isolate the optical transition without the signal to expose problems;
   this proof is NOT a completed shop migration. Then integrate its approved
   alien-head signal and check the final result in context.
3. **Meaning exchange, cuts 33/34:** two moving picture frames plus one continuous
   airborne object crossing the boundary. Preserve both source clocks, loop,
   apparent depth and payload; no resetting animation at the edit.
4. **Outro TV:** per-take planar registration, soft screen masks, bright-static
   suppression, face-flash-synchronous burst, and additive collapse to a point.
   Earlier receiver mapping proves the basic method, not these specific plates.
5. **Outro projected room light and particle continuity:** apparent wall/furniture
   depth, additive cores, spatially coherent colors, accelerating inward motion
   and later settling tail. Existing native airflow proves the particle method;
   it does not prove these projection and cross-cut continuity requirements.
6. **Night exit:** noisy elongated water reflection in perspective, distant green
   actor and bloom, soft optical iris. Test small distant lights and edge softness
   at both sizes, with no final grain/mastering baked in.
7. **Remaining scope and globe variants:** inspect saved dependencies. Reuse proven
   analog paths/persistence where possible, but explicitly test native scope
   display and rapid three-dimensional globe spin/temporal sampling. An existing
   Blender wrapper around raster FX is not a native migration.

These are technique families, not a request for seven separate approval stops.
Work in dependency order where helpful; cut 29's optical proof can be done before
cut 26's full procedural world because it is small and isolates resampling risks.

## Batch after exceptional proofs

Reuse tested native light, tracked highlight, particle, atmospheric, rosette,
crop and text methods on remaining instances. Review internally and deliver
grouped contextual previews. Stop for owner input only for a material creative
choice, missing source/authority, or a visible change that cannot be resolved
without changing the accepted intent. Do not wait just because another ordinary
shot has finished.

## Checks and acceptance

- Exact song frames and timestamp-preserving source frames; no new audio offsets.
- Raw plates only in saved scenes. No old baked FX hidden behind native labels.
- Check transition entry, switch/peak and exit, including random-access rerenders.
- Render representative 1080 frames; compare after downsampling for framing,
  mask edges, blur size, small lights and particle density.
- Preserve original approved looks as references, not mandatory pixel identity.
- Distinguish technique proof, complete shot migration and full assembly release.
  Full-film preflight remains closed until every dependency is resolved.

The owner accepted waking cut 20 before this workflow change. It should not be
sent through another approval cycle.
