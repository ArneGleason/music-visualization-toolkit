## Resolution and mastering

720 previews are disposable Blender caches. A 1080 delivery must rerender the
native effect scenes at 1920x1080, then relink those caches in the Blender edit.
Simply enlarging the 720 assembly would also enlarge its cached effects and is
NOT the mastering workflow. The saved 1920 scenes are resolution test scenes,
not full 1080 deliveries yet. Raw video upscaling remains a separate, future
stage using only selected source spans. No paid upscaling, grain, global grade
or final mastering has been performed or baked into these elements.

`full_native_review_draft.json` is isolated from production. `full_selection_check.json`
checks every cut against the unchanged migration timing grid. Dependency
inspection and receipts must be reconciled before full native completion is claimed.

## Full assembly color boundary

The first full render exposed an extra AgX display transform at the assembly
boundary. It reduced contrast and bright FX energy even though every source
and inpoint was correct. This is not an accepted creative change. The saved
full assembly and its reproducible wrapper now use Standard / None, exposure
0 and gamma 1. Individual caches retain their own accepted looks. The full
preview is rerendered and checked against those cache frames, with no change
to the effect scenes or timing. Keep this guard in the future 1080 assembly.
