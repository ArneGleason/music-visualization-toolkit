## Continuity method
Use 10-15 second ongoing performances for return cuts, overlapping scene time with a separate reaction/insert angle. Keep screen direction, prop hand, creature position and action phase explicit. Reference Elements support identity, not deterministic shared choreography. Do not assume independent takes will match their movements. Keep editorial handles. Break actions into stages when a long take fails.
