# Experimental projective depth v003

Footage mapped with projective UVs onto a 96x54 quad curved depth shell. Distance is greatest near normalized source moon region (0.24, 0.85), decreasing toward lower/peripheral image areas. Perspective camera translates with the existing two-beat bob and four-beat sway. Off-axis camera shift compensates at the distant 20-unit plane; near vertices therefore move more. Underlying generated video movement remains. This is an artistic continuous depth approximation, not object-separated depth or a solved scene; it cannot reveal hidden surfaces and may subtly bend architecture.

Approved lens distortion 0.10 and dispersion 0.002 retained. Roll removed to avoid rotating the distance. 7% framing overscan retained via camera focal length. Source timing unchanged.
