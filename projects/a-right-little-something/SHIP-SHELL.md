# Active transparent shell

The sphere is an ultra-thin, extremely transparent and durable technological material. Apparent thickness in illustrations can come from edge highlights and internal reflections; a thick double wall is not part of the design. The interior surface can project imagery and magnified telescope views while preserving the impression of looking directly through the sphere.

An optional glowing geodesic network can appear as an in-world display, with adjustable visibility and brightness. Its cells are predominantly hexagonal; twelve pentagonal closures accommodate the spherical topology. The network follows the curved sphere, rather than using flat panels, and is not a set of structural window frames.

The approved appearance is a very transparent field-like shell with faint cyan geodesic lines when useful, rather than shiny refractive glass. Network visibility can vary with the shot or technological activity. Interior views avoid invented glass perimeters; exterior views may retain a subtle spherical silhouette and field glow without a bubbly lens effect.
