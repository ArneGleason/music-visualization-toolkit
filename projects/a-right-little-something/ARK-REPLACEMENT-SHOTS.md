## Continuity review

All four candidates show six lateral pods, a central ivory/graphite spine,
cyan galleries and a vertical aft field ring. Habitats use muted terrain-scale
variation rather than saturated rainbow colors or oversized flora.

These are generated angles, not views of a measured 3D model. In lab2-07 some
habitat contents vary from the reference's physical biome order. In lab2-08
individual organisms are too small to verify. Lab2-09 retains six pods but the
ship occupies about 26% of the frame, rather than the requested 16–18%, and the
hull appears more compact than the reference. These limitations remain visible
for user review; they are not claimed to be exact geometry matches. Failed first
attempts and exact final prompts are preserved alongside the assets.
