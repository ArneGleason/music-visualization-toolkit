## V04: quieter palette and larger habitat scale

The user found V03 too rainbow-like and its organisms too large, making the
pods look small. Latest candidate:
`generated/design/ark-biopods-v04/ark-biopods-design-v04.png`.
Built-in ImageGen edited V03's habitat interiors. Exact prompt and QA metadata
are in the same folder as `generation-prompt.txt` and `generation-prompt.json`.
Board item `lab-ark-biopods-concept-v04`. The user accepted V04 and requested
replacement exterior starter frames on October 10. Four review drafts and
their motion directions are documented in `ARK-REPLACEMENT-SHOTS.md`; review
batch `ark-replacement-v01`. Replacement Kling video is not yet authorized.

Six habitats now share muted sage/olive, slate teal, mineral and earth tones,
with small plum/ochre variations. Biological glow and saturated color washes
are reduced. Alien flora forms much smaller scattered colonies amid broad
terrain, waterways, basins, ridges and distant haze. Clear atmospheric space
above the organisms and receding landscape layers make the pods read as vast
habitat volumes. Differences come from terrain and ecology as well as subtle
color. The six-pod layout, hull, supports, field ring and established ship lights
are visually retained. V33 previews the four new exterior stills at the existing
phrase timings; previous native exterior footage remains archived.

Keep the V03 physical biome order, interpreted in this muted landscape palette:
port forward branching groves; port middle fungal wetland; port aft dry spiral
organism fields; starboard forward water/frond basin; starboard middle membranous
marsh; starboard aft curled-leaf plains. Use V04 as the approved design reference,
with small organisms and broad terrain as continuity requirements for later angles.

User direction: pods should spread laterally on one level, three on each side.
The middle pair reaches farther outboard, suggesting a ring of six around the
central ship. The body should be much more futuristic and use a field/warp-like
drive related to the ring on Harper's personal vessel, replacing rear rockets.

The reference has a sleek ivory/graphite centerline laboratory hull with cyan
oval culture galleries, smoothly curved connecting arms, three lateral pod
pairs and one vertical cyan aft field-drive ring with amber outer edge. The
forward pair contains forests, middle pair wetlands/shallow water, rear pair
dryland. All pod bases share one structural level; their screen elevations
differ with perspective. The broad arrangement has visible space between
the modules and spine. Exposed trusses, cargo drums and rocket barrels are gone.

For future shot references, preserve six-pod count, left/right paired layout,
outboard middle-pair spacing, biome order, continuous centerline hull, arm
geometry, equal base level, vertical aft drive ring, and material/light palette.
This replaces V01's three upper pods as the proposed design, pending approval.

## V01: three upper pods, initial industrial candidate

The user requested one modern Silent Running-inspired robot headquarters ship
design reference to approve before applying it to shots. Generated with built-in

The active v30 animatic and accepted spacecraft shots remain as reviewed.

Creative Production board: `ea37232e-e258-4176-b1b9-1d38747fc88e`;
item: `lab-ark-biopods-concept-v01`.

The design combines an exposed graphite triangular-truss spine with an ivory
forward laboratory capsule. Cyan oval gallery windows carry over the tank/lab
visual language. Three separate geodesic domes sit on circular bases above the
spine: forest forward, wetland middle, dryland aft. Articulated service arms
surround the mounts; radiator panels and utility tanks sit below. The compact
aft drive has three vertically stacked cyan exhaust apertures. Amber utility
lighting and the painterly graphic-novel finish connect it to the existing film.

For later views, preserve pod count, relative sizes, front-to-rear biome order,
mount spacing, laboratory capsule, drive arrangement, materials and light palette.
The reference shows the laboratory at left and drive at right; those screen
positions may reverse with the camera while the physical layout remains fixed.
Alternate-angle stills should be checked against this anchor before generating
replacement footage. A single view establishes visible design, while reverse
and underside geometry will need to be resolved if those views are requested.
