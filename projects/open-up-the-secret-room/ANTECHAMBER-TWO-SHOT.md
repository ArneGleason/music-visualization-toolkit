## Composition proposal

Chest-up two-shot, camera ahead and slightly to one side of the pair, looking
back across the entrance hall. Keep both faces readable in three-quarter view,
with a shared eyeline rather than one face hidden behind a shoulder or reduced
to full profile. Their eyes can meet without extreme head turns. Preserve their
relative height and build. Background ribs, recesses and sparse amber activation
carry the old entrance's materials inside without competing with expressions.
The geography/camera is a proposal until the selected facial references are set.

Expression: curious, bewildered discovery and a shared 'are you seeing this?'
moment. Avoid a generic grin, panic, exaggerated cartoon surprise or identical
expressions. Do not copy the alarm/sarcasm of an old reference as the new acting.
