## Compact pod revision — Image 27

Image26 was too stark and spacious. The capsule should have just enough room
for three occupants and awkward boarding. [Image27](generated/world-concepts-v005/image-27.png)
brings the inward-facing seats together, reduces the open floor to a narrow
footwell, and adds connected living membranes, luminous channels and integrated
equipment through the walls and floor. Maintain functional industrial geometry
with organic infrastructure, not decorative fantasy ornament. Candidate pending
review. Actual occupant fit remains to be tested; animatic unchanged.

## Shared constraints

Image 16 anchors graphic-novel rendering, dark mineral architecture and sparse
amber lighting. The living species can add localized luminous color while
leaving substantial shadow. Existing character references govern the ant-like
species' beak, antennae and four-arm/two-leg anatomy. No new Harper or Taffy
designs are needed for these object and species studies.

Both species have language, agency and their own technological traditions.
The living species constructs through its bodies and language; the ant-like
people manipulate raw materials. Their technology is jointly invented. Do not
depict the living collaborators as pets, slaves, livestock, or machines that
simply obey a controller. Exactly how individual organisms connect into larger
structures remains open; a drawing of shared infrastructure must not silently
settle their biology or social organization.

The orbital model provisionally depicts two moons around a banded parent planet,
with a distinctive blue crescent destination and one clear route. Repeat shape,
placement and orientation as well as color. This is a visual storytelling device,
not an astronomical scale diagram or a commitment to named worlds.

## Review and next steps

Choose or refine the living species before repeating it widely. Compare its
standalone image with the shared workshop to see whether identity and agency
survive a more complex scene. Compare the model with its pod installation to
see whether the destination remains recognizable at a smaller scale.

Other useful later studies include the concealment breach/control, the council
with Understanding's empty chair, and the concealed launch outlet. These are
deferred from this first group so that the central design language can settle.
