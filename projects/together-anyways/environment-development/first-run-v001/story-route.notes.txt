ONE WALKABLE DISTRICT, FOUR SETTING REFERENCES
A / market approach (loc-market-approach):
A covered alcove beside a public market lane provides a planning place. Its
sightline reaches the criminal's teal shade and twin blue tanks. Old sandstone,
shade repairs and salvaged goods mix with a practical information display.
That first display carries ordinary useful local information, not a wanted
alert. A distant elevated cargo rail/crane plants the eventual port objective.

B / water broker's house-front depot (loc-water-broker):
A public-facing stall and forecourt attached to the racketeer's private house.
His chair and private water supply communicate local power. Shade, pumps,
locked blue tanks, a trough, a barred storage gate and room for associates
provide more staging than a single chair in an undefined alley.
Harper enters the conspicuous public side. Taffy stays screened near the chair's
blind side. Her jewel draws the racketeer, associates and shoulder lookout away
from his work. The asymmetric horseshoe override key remains the object stolen.
Harper's public decoy exit and Taffy's narrow maintenance exit must be distinct.
The barred gate here belongs to this depot, not to the next secure destination.

C / service court beneath a blue pipe elbow (loc-reunion-court):
A shallow old spillway and pump-service landing behind the street. A higher
screened ledge connects the boy's short covert exit; a lower dogleg passage
lets Harper lose the people following her. Piers and angles break sightlines
to the meeting point. Both routes converge under a chipped blue pipe elbow,
a memorable recurring landmark. An eastward stair/ramp leads to the next door.
Use the first Are you with me? as checking whether he made it, then Together
anyways as rejoining. Do not put this reunion after the later news reveal.

D / maintenance door into a freight relay hall (loc-relay-gate, loc-relay-hall):
A secondary entrance to a port-serving freight/utility facility. The key grants
one next step, not direct entry to a spacecraft. A physical ceramic/bronze reader
with an asymmetric horseshoe socket makes the device's purpose visually clear.
It sits low enough for the boy to use. He can initially orient it wrongly, then
understand the longer prong and turn it. This operation is a proposal, not yet
an approved prop-mechanism lock. No new stolen object or second theft is needed.
The doorway is the access boundary; no convenient open side route bypasses it.
Inside, freight rails and an onward ramp suggest a route toward cargo gantries.
A large flat display is INSIDE the hall. They cross the threshold, then see
their own predicament on it. Wanted portraits/text will be composed separately
from exact character references after identity selection; environment concept
screens intentionally carry no invented faces or unreliable generated writing.

ENVIRONMENT IMAGE POLICY
Four clean unpopulated location studies, not final storyboard shots. Empty
staging space lets us place the established children, racketeer, associates and
lookout consistently later. Cover governs city materials and racketeer setting;
its old boy costume does not override the latest character design.
Shared palette: warm stone and mineral plaster, chipped blue tank/door paint,
teal/ochre shade, rusted metal, exposed utility plumbing, selective maintained
ceramic/electronic inserts. Avoid uniformly futuristic gloss or generic fantasy
bazaar dressing. These are proposed designs; reconcile camera-specific exits
against the route map before treating them as production geography.
