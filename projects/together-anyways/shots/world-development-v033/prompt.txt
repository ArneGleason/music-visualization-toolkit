Roughly small motorhome cabin volume, not giant airliner. A single starboard personnel door forward of intake, just behind two-seat cockpit, with folding steps provides clear scale. Continuous cabin forward of machinery bulkhead; intake ducts and machinery aft, not blocking walkway. Grounded landing gear: centerline nose wheel and aft main bogie plus short wing outriggers. Three-quarter FRONT STARBOARD view, moderately elevated, nose toward lower right, enough side visible to inspect two near-side rotating nozzles and short wing, far-side hardware naturally occluded. Full vehicle uncropped with breathing room on neutral warm gray ground and backdrop. Realistic illustrated cinematic concept art, dimensional materials and believable connected surfaces, patchy aged ivory paint with faded teal areas, tarnished metal, dusty shabby salvaged craft. Mechanical detail restrained so structural forms read clearly. No weapons, military markings, missiles, fuel tanks under wings, text, logos, people, extra engine pods, multiple views or inset panels.
