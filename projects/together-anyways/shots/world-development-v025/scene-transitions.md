## Within scenes

Default to cuts. A scene break alone does not require a fade: the action continuing across it is more important. The main additional dissolve is six frames into S10-SH04, from extracting the donor module to installing it, implying the return trip and omitted work.

Keep the short S09-SH04 “True?” as an insert, not a new scene. Keep the taps through L36–L38 as one continuous shot. In S11, cut into and out of the news feed to make the scrapper’s removal causal. In S12, cut between repeated code attempts and the second terminal; establish its new surroundings in the composition. Keep clearance trigger → failed port controls → takeoff as direct cuts.

In S13, cut from the hopeful high view to the failing part, then the descent. In S14, dust can naturally obscure the crash and bridge into the cabin; that is motivated occlusion, not a separate decorative dissolve. Leave the last cave image through the musical end; do not spend the final reveal time on a long fade.

When footage exists, use real 24-frame in/out handles for the proposed dissolves, centered on existing cut boundaries. They do not extend the song or alter shot duration. Final cut/fade decisions remain subject to playback review.
