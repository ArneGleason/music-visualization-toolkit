## Emotional direction

something together, rather than merely had a pleasant chat. Relaxed shoulders,
focused eyes, a small exhale, a brief private half-smile at most. Not broad
grinning, delighted surprise, coy posing or bouncy delivery. Not dour, vacant
or exhausted either. Preserve personality, dry humor and clear intentional
speech/singing. Do not solve excessive happiness by asking for a closed mouth
or a motionless face: earlier tests showed that deadpan base acting harmed
lip-sync results. Natural articulation and visible teeth when appropriate,
not continuously displayed teeth or exaggerated whitening.

## Screen technique and scope

Reuse the approved analog scope vocabulary: amber/cyan traces, uneven beam
weight, bloom, phosphor persistence and restrained noise. Use three authored
states: restless trace, coherent double-loop/encoded form, quiet residual
signal. Motion/brightness derives from the exact song interval and deliberate
dialogue accents, not arbitrary oscillation. The motif can recall the earlier
world/receiver exchange without replaying that whole effect.

Start with a locked or very restrained camera, screen unobscured. Track its
four corners or planar region, mask the curved interior, retain glass and
bezel occlusion. No full-scene 3D solve required for the initial test. Keep
graphics in separate clean layers and apply once; no filmic mastering here.
Keep bloom modest enough that added light does not demand implausible changes
to the whole face/room. Test one screen moment before programming all lines.
